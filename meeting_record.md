# Meeting Record

## Summary
The speaker argues that mathematical research should reward not only new results but also the clarity and explanatory power of proofs, proposing prizes for outstanding exposition. He reflects on the role of heroism in motivating mathematicians, worries about a future where only computational achievements remain, and suggests extending competitive math into adulthood as a way to preserve heroism. He also speculates on the potential of machine-generated proofs to reveal emergent meta‑mathematical phenomena.
